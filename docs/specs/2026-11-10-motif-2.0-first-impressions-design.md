# Motif 2.0: first impressions that win

Status: approved by the owner on 2026-11-10. Follows the 2.0 design (`2026-11-08-motif-2.0-character-and-library-design.md`); nothing here undoes it.

## Why

Test A on run 20261109-081330 failed: the owner picked Motif 42% of the time (bar: more than 50%), the AI judges 75% / 17% baseline (bar: at least 70% / at most 15%). The baseline was plain Opus 5.5 with no plugins or personal skills (the harness checks this); it used only the built-in `dataviz` skill, once.

The baseline won on first-impression screens (sign-in, welcome) and lost on structure (desktop layouts, home detail). Side by side, the baseline used 4–6 colours, layered gradients and glow, display type of weight 800–900 at 62–74 px, and a recurring detail per app (hard offset shadows, tilted badges). Motif's own rules ruled most of that out: one brand colour in the contract, "never a gradient backdrop", display capped at 36–52 px, "a sign-in has one button", "one quiet item" on a business sign-in panel, and 39 "never" lines in `character.md`, many written from AI-judge feedback.

Two Motif text bugs also lost votes: Ledgerly's "18 / days / late" wrapping one word per line, and Stride's "Thursday Tempo" cut to "F…".

## Principles this follows

Each change rests on general design guidance, not one person's taste:

- **Colour hierarchy:** one dominant colour, a supporting one, small accents (the 60-30-10 rule of thumb). Colourfulness helps first impressions a little; clutter hurts a lot.
- **Depth shows hierarchy** (Material Design elevation, Apple's layering): depth marks what matters, not every surface.
- **Type scale:** first-impression headlines are a step above in-app display (Material's largest display style is 57; web heroes commonly run 48–72).
- **Form usability:** fewer controls means fewer errors. Richness goes in the panel and the form's craft, never in controls the app doesn't have.
- **Familiar layouts, distinctive surfaces:** people prefer layouts that look familiar and simple; uniqueness lives in drawing, type, colour and one signature detail.
- **Reflow and text spacing (WCAG 1.4.10, 1.4.12):** text is never cut off or broken apart.
- **Positive direction beats rule lists** (Anthropic's frontend-design guidance: commit to a direction, spend boldness in one place, create depth rather than flat fills).

## Changes

### 1. Two accent colours in the contract

New optional colour roles `accent` and `accent-2`, each with a dark value, in every preset. They are mid-light hues from the brand's neighbourhood or its complement, chosen so `text-primary` reads on them at 4.5:1 (pairs `text-primary` on `accent`, `text-primary` on `accent-2`, kind `text`).

- **For:** illustration fills, kind tags and their dots, icon tiles, avatar fills, chart series after the first, numbered step badges.
- **Never:** buttons, links or focus (those stay `action-primary` and `focus`), large backgrounds.
- **Area cap:** on any screen, the brand fill plus both accents cover at most about a third of the screen; each accent alone stays small (about a tenth).
- Generated theme code exposes them on all nine stacks like the brand roles. `motif validate`, the preview kit and `motif check` treat them like `brand-soft`.

### 2. A hero type role

New optional font role `hero`: the headline of a welcome, landing or sign-in screen only. Each preset sets it a clear step above `display` (56–72 px on the web, scaled for phones), with the weight the style calls for: the heaviest weight the font has for Bold, Playful and energetic briefs; the display weight for calm ones. Tight tracking (about -0.02em) at that size. `display` stays as it is for in-app key figures.

### 3. Depth pieces in the library

A new library group, `depth`, with three pieces written for all nine stacks, built only from theme roles so dark mode and `motif check` hold:

- **Tonal gradient:** two neighbouring shades of `brand` (mixed with `surface` or `text-primary`), on the sign-in panel or the hero band only.
- **Soft glow:** a large, blurred `brand-soft` or `accent` circle behind the hero's drawing or the panel's preview.
- **Pattern:** a faint repeat (diagonal lines, dots, a topographic line) at 5–8% opacity on a brand panel.

At most one depth piece per screen, on first-impression screens only. Working screens stay flat, as `visual-craft.md` says.

### 4. Sign-in pieces

A new library piece, `screens/sign-in.md`, all nine stacks:

- **Rich panel (desktop):** the brand panel holds a layered preview of the product (two or three of its real cards, slightly offset, the front one raised) or the brief's drawing, plus a large quote in `title` or `hero` size with a name. A depth piece may sit behind it.
- **Crafted form:** the form in a raised card or straight on the page, with the `hero` headline, generous spacing, leading icons in fields, and only the sign-in methods the app really has (no invented Google button or tabs).
- **Phone:** the drawing or mark at the top, then the form; one depth piece behind the drawing at most.

`character.md`'s sign-in paragraph and `ux-patterns/sign-in.md` point to the piece. The "one quiet item" limit on business panels goes; the panel stays calm in colour (no red overdue lists).

### 5. One signature detail per style

Each of the eight accent files gains a **Signature** section: one small detail used the same way on buttons, cards and badges across the app. Examples: Playful's hard offset shadow and slight tilt on badges; Bold's heavy outline; Editorial's hairline rule and italic accent word; Dark tech's thin glow edge. On by default for Playful, Bold and Dark tech; optional for the rest. DESIGN.md records the chosen signature in section 2.

### 6. The C look, narrowed

`anti-patterns.md` keeps banning the heavy version: a gradient behind everything, glowing charts, tilted or glass cards on working screens, colour on every surface. It now allows, on first-impression screens only: one depth piece (change 3); a glass card on Dark tech's panel; a slight tilt on badges for Playful and Bold. "Gradient overuse" and "Glass and glow" are reworded to match.

### 7. First-impression guidance rewritten as recipes

`character.md`'s "Building a brand moment" becomes positive recipes per screen (welcome, landing, sign-in): what goes where, in what order, at what size, and which piece to use. The "never" lines that apply only to working screens move to a "Working screens" section; lines that repeat the slip list are merged. Two checks are added to "Before you finish":

- **Squint test:** blur the screenshot; the headline, the main button and the one brand moment still stand out, and nothing else competes.
- **Clutter limit:** at most one depth piece, one drawing and the accent area cap per screen.

No line names a test brief or its content (the rule against writing skill text aimed at the test).

### 8. Text-fit check

A new `motif shot --audit` rule, `text-fit`, on the web audit and the Flutter, Compose and SwiftUI harnesses (React Native through its web build, as today):

- **Cut off:** text whose content is wider than its box with an ellipsis or clip (web: `scrollWidth > clientWidth` with `text-overflow: ellipsis` or `overflow: hidden`; native: the platform's truncation or overflow flag).
- **Broken apart:** a short label of three words or fewer laid out one word per line.

Severity major on headings, buttons, chips and card titles; minor elsewhere. The finding says what was cut and suggests shorter copy or letting the row wrap once.

### 9. Quick vote before the full run

Before the 10-hour Test A, run `--tests A --cases` for three briefs (one energetic, one business, one gentle) and have the owner vote on their sign-in and welcome pairs by phone (about 12 pairs, 15 minutes). This is a check, not the gate: the full Test A decides.

Outside voters (2–3 people beside the owner) were suggested; the owner has not decided. The pass bar (decision 0014) stays as it is until he does.

## Not changing

- The four rules, roles-only, and `motif check`.
- B/C/D tests, fixtures and graders (rule 4 of the release).
- Working-screen rules in `visual-craft.md`.
- The 2.0 library pieces, except where change 4 adds a link.
